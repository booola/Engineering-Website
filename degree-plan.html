Using the flowchart

How to read it

The chart reads left to right. A course sits to the right of everything it requires, and rows group related subjects so you can follow each thread.

  1. Follow the arrows

    Each solid arrow points from a prerequisite to the course that requires it. Gold dotted lines mark co-requisites, which can be taken in the same term or earlier.

  2. Follow the rows

    Rows group subjects: engineering foundations and capstone, mechanics and design, mathematics, physics, chemistry, and environmental studies.

  3. Hover to trace a path

    Hovering a course highlights everything that leads to it and everything it opens. The rest of the chart fades back.

  4. Select to pin

    Click or tap a course to keep its card open. From the card, open the course's page in the Centre catalog.

  5. See your progress

    Courses you mark complete on the Checklist page show a check mark here, so you can see what's open to you next.

  6. Filter the view

    Use the buttons above the chart to show only required courses or only elective options. On a keyboard, Tab moves between courses, Enter pins a card, and Escape closes it.

Rules to know

Fine print

  • PHY 370 and CHE 361 count once. If you use one of them as a core elective, you can't use the other as your second core elective.
  • Unused core electives become technical electives. Any core elective not used for the core group can count toward the four technical electives.
  • Any EGR 440–449 design course can fill the design elective. EGR 450 requires a design elective as a co-requisite.
  • Chemistry: CHE 131 or CHE 135. Students who complete CHE 135 can't register for CHE 131 or CHE 132, and the reverse. CHE 361 requires CHE 132 or CHE 135.
  • Some upper-level physics is offered in alternate years: PHY 340, PHY 350, PHY 370, and PHY 380. Plan ahead.
